# User Manual — GlowCart

## 1. Customer Guide
1. **Create an Account & Sign In:** Visit `/register` to create a customer account, then log in at `/login`.
2. **Browse, Search & Filter:** Use the top search bar or `/catalog` filters (category, brand, skin type, shade/color, price range, availability, sorting).
3. **Select Shades & Volumes:** Open any product page and click a shade swatch or milliliter chip. The SKU, price, discount, and live stock update immediately.
4. **Cart & Wishlist:** Click **Add to Cart** or the heart icon to save to `/wishlist`. On `/cart`, adjust quantities or apply a coupon (e.g., `GLOW15`).
5. **Checkout & Payment:** On `/checkout`, select or add a delivery address, choose **Cash on Delivery** or **Online Payment Sandbox**, and confirm purchase.
6. **Track Orders & Submit Reviews:** View order status timelines at `/orders`, cancel eligible pending orders, submit verified-purchase reviews on product pages, and message support at `/support`.

## 2. Administrator Guide
1. **Sign In:** Access `/admin/login` with an `admin` or `super_admin` account.
2. **Catalog & Variants:** Manage categories, brands, products, and shade/volume SKUs from the left sidebar.
3. **Inventory, Batches & Expiry:** Use **Batches & Expiry** to receive cosmetic stock batches with manufacture and expiry dates. Expired batches are automatically blocked from sale; FEFO allocates the earliest expiring valid batch first.
4. **Orders & Payments:** Update order statuses (`CONFIRMED → PROCESSING → SHIPPED → DELIVERED`), inspect payment transactions, and issue refunds where applicable.
5. **Promotions, Reviews & Support:** Launch promotional coupons, moderate customer reviews, and reply to support tickets.
6. **Analytics & Reports:** Inspect real-time KPI cards and Recharts charts on `/admin` and export CSV reports under `/admin/reports`.

## 3. Super Admin Guide
- **Admin Accounts:** Navigate to `/admin/customers` to create new `admin` accounts.
- **Audit Logs & Settings:** Navigate to `/admin/settings` to inspect immutable `audit_logs` and store configuration.
