# User Stories — GlowCart

## Customer User Stories

- **US-001 (Registration):** As a visitor, I want to register an account with email and password so that I can save addresses, manage a cart, and place orders.
- **US-002 (Login & Refresh):** As a customer, I want to log in securely and stay signed in via token refresh so that my shopping session persists safely.
- **US-003 (Browse & Filter):** As a customer, I want to browse products by category, brand, price range, skin type, shade/color, and availability so that I find suitable cosmetics quickly.
- **US-004 (Search):** As a customer, I want to search cosmetics by product name or brand so that I can jump directly to desired items.
- **US-005 (Variant Selection):** As a customer, I want to select a foundation shade or skincare volume on the product page and immediately see its SKU, price, discount, and stock status.
- **US-006 (Shopping Cart & Coupons):** As a customer, I want to add/update/remove cart items and apply valid coupon codes so that I see accurate subtotals, discounts, shipping, and total cost.
- **US-007 (Wishlist):** As a customer, I want to save favorite cosmetic products to my wishlist for later purchase.
- **US-008 (Checkout & Payment):** As a customer, I want to choose a delivery address, pick Cash on Delivery or Online Payment Sandbox, and confirm my order safely without duplicate charges.
- **US-009 (Order Tracking & Cancellation):** As a customer, I want to view my order history, track order/payment status, and cancel eligible pending orders.
- **US-010 (Reviews & Support):** As a customer, I want to submit/edit/delete verified-purchase reviews and send support messages to administrators.

## Admin User Stories

- **US-011 (Catalog & Variant Management):** As an admin, I want to create and update categories, brands, products, images, and shade/volume variants so that the storefront remains current.
- **US-012 (Batch & FEFO Inventory Management):** As an admin, I want to receive stock batches with manufacture/expiry dates, view low-stock and near-expiry alerts, and prevent expired batches from being sold.
- **US-013 (Order & Payment Operations):** As an admin, I want to inspect orders, transition order statuses through valid states, collect COD or refund payments, and ensure stock updates stay accurate.
- **US-014 (Sales & Variant Analytics):** As an admin, I want to view real-time revenue charts, category/brand performance, and best-selling lipstick/foundation shades so that I can optimize inventory decisions.
- **US-015 (Moderation & Customer Service):** As an admin, I want to moderate reviews, manage promotions/coupons, and reply to customer messages.

## Super Admin User Stories

- **US-016 (User & Role Administration):** As a super admin, I want to create admin accounts, assign roles/permissions, configure system settings, and review immutable audit logs.
